FAQ

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything is processed on your iPhone.

I can't save my image.
Open this app in the iOS Settings app and allow adding to Photos.

I want to compare with the original photo.
Press and hold the image to see the original photo. Tap to view it full screen, and pinch to zoom in.

I made a mistake while adjusting.
Tap the Undo button at the top left to step back one change at a time. Double-tap a slider to reset it to its default value.

It takes a few seconds to make a depth map.
The AI runs on your iPhone, so it takes a few seconds per photo. On older iPhones with less memory, the app automatically switches to a lighter AI model.
