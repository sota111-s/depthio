Privacy Policy

Depthio / Last updated: October 1, 2026

Depthio ("the App") creates depth maps from your photos. The App does not collect any of your data.

1. Information we collect

The App never sends personal information, photos, usage data, or device information to the developer or to any third party. It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

2. How your photos are handled

Photos you choose are processed entirely on your iPhone, only to estimate depth and create images. They are never uploaded to the internet. The App does not use the network.

So that you can reopen your works later, the App keeps a reduced-size copy of each photo you choose and its depth data inside the App on your iPhone. They are removed when you delete the work in the App or delete the App itself. If you use iPhone backups (such as iCloud), they may be included in your backup through Apple's backup system.

3. Photo library access

You pick photos with the iOS photo picker, so the App can read only the photos you select. The App asks for permission to add images to your photo library only when you save a result. You can withdraw this permission at any time in the iOS Settings app.

4. Sharing with third parties

Because we do not collect any information, we have nothing to share with third parties.

5. Children

Because the App does not collect information, it does not collect information from children either.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
